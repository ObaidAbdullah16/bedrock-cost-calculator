# Using the calculator to start client conversations

The calculator is the free step before the paid **GenAI Cost & Safety Check** (S6) and the
**AWS Bill Doctor** (S1).

1. **Post it.** A LinkedIn post with one real-looking example: "A support bot on Haiku 4.5 that the
   pricing page says costs $326/month really costs about $2,831: Guardrails on the whole prompt,
   an OpenSearch minimum, full chat history." Link to the calculator, not to a sales page.
2. **Answer questions with it.** In communities (r/aws, AWS user groups, Indie Hackers), when
   someone asks "why is my Bedrock bill so high", reply with the 3 most likely causes and a share
   link pre-filled with their numbers. Help first; don't pitch.
3. **Signal outreach.** Companies hiring for "GenAI" or using Bedrock in public repos: "I ran your
   kind of setup through my free calculator. The usual surprise is X. Happy to look at yours in 15 minutes."
4. **Proposal attachment.** Paste "Copy summary" into the proposal: it shows the before, and the
   savings ideas become the scope.

Never present an estimate as their actual bill. It is a model of their setup; their AWS Cost
Explorer is the truth, and the paid check starts there.
